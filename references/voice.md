## How to fill this

Paste 1-2 things written recently, **raw and unedited**:

- A WhatsApp message to a client
- An email to a prospect or partner
- A LinkedIn post

Unedited samples beat anything typed fresh into a chat, because text written mid-conversation is
already shaped by the conversation. Drop them here or in `Transition/` and this file gets built
from them.
